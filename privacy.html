Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

z440-backup is used only by its owner, to store encrypted backup files from a home server in the owner's own Google Drive.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive permission limited to files it creates itself (drive.file). It cannot see or change any other file in the Drive.

What it stores

Backup data is encrypted on the home server before upload. Nothing is sent to, stored by, or shared with any third party, and no analytics or tracking is used.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps.
